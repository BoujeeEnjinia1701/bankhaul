# BankHaul regulatory and standards register

No product certification is known to apply to a hand-worked rope station, but every installation needs the fisheries authority's agreement that loop-set gillnets are allowed there (G1), the wildlife authority's agreement on siting and safety (G2) and possibly a permit for works on the shore and a line across the water (G3, G4). The steel, rigging and rope standards below are the reference for quality when parts are bought. Not legal advice; anything marked "to assess" needs a qualified reviewer, and the jurisdiction depends on the co-design partner (first candidate on Lake Kariba, Zimbabwe; BKH-DDR-001, item 10). Method: STANDARDS section 23.
