# BankHaul project plan

BankHaul is at stage P3 (design outputs), TRL 3. The pipeline documents, the D3 models of the three concept options, the calculation note, the drawings, the priced BOM and the build plan are done for option A, chosen by Amish on 2026-10-25 (BKH-DEC-001 O-1). The next gate, P4 (verification, TRL 4), is on hold while the portfolio stage cap is P3, and needs a co-design partner and a trial site first. Method: STANDARDS sections 19 and 23.

## Next steps and decision requested

Amish decided O-1 to O-8 on 2026-10-25 as recommended (BKH-DEC-001): option A, the bank-edge board (measured in the R3 trial), the B and C fixes on paper only, the site render as hero, the duplicate record marked superseded, the indicative block price kept and confirmed locally, the lowering rope for the far stake, and the 3.5 m drive kept. The recommended next step is to approach the co-design partner; when the cap allows, TRL 4 builds one steel station and runs the R4, R5 and R10 proof loads.
