# BankHaul market and viability

BankHaul is for small-scale fishers in crocodile water and the associations, wildlife authorities and NGOs that support them. No commercial product that sets and hauls a gillnet from the bank was found, so the benchmarks are the parts it is made of and the main existing mitigation, crocodile exclusion enclosures, which protect water collection rather than fishing. The steel trial station costs USD 399 in parts; the local-materials station that PR-008 aims at is estimated at USD 98 against USD 80. Prices were checked on 2026-10-25. Method: STANDARDS section 23.

## Open route

A fisher association or an NGO builds stations with a local welder, carpenter and rope worker from the drawings and build plan; the wildlife authority helps site them where its attack records point; researchers measure the effect. CERN-OHL-S keeps changes open. The steel station is the trial version; the local-materials station is what spreads, once the partner has costed it (PR-008).
