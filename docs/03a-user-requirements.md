# BankHaul user requirements

What users need, written from their side. The fourteen needs were drawn on 2026-10-25 from the users and stakeholders in BKH-PRB-001 (Tables 2 and 3) and the original requirements R1 to R11; they score the concept options (BKH-OPT-001), and the product requirements that meet them are in BKH-PRS-001 (`03-requirements.md`), linked in the traceability matrix BKH-RTM-001 (`03b-traceability.md`). They remain a desk draft until a co-design partner reviews them with fishers at a trial site. Method: STANDARDS section 22.

Table 1. User requirements.

| ID | Category | Title | Need | Priority | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UR-001 | Safety | Never wade | I can set, check and haul my net without stepping into the water | High | Subsistence fisher (PRB Table 2) |
| UR-002 | Performance | Use our nets | I can use the gillnet I already have, with small changes at its ends | High | Subsistence fisher |
| UR-003 | Safety | Stay back from the edge | I can do the whole job standing well back from the water's edge | High | Fisher; wildlife authority (PRB Table 3) |
| UR-004 | Usability | One person can haul | One person can haul a net with a night's catch without straining | High | Subsistence fisher |
| UR-005 | Performance | Reach the fish | The net reaches 30 m out, where the fish are | Medium | Subsistence fisher |
| UR-006 | Safety | A seized net lets go | If a crocodile or a snag takes the net, it cannot pull me or the station in | High | Fisher; wildlife authority |
| UR-007 | Usability | Fits a fishing night | Setting and hauling take minutes, not an hour | Medium | Subsistence fisher |
| UR-008 | Environment and durability | Stays put | The bank post and the far stake hold in soft, wet banks and muddy beds | High | Fisher association |
| UR-009 | Environment and durability | Lasts a season | It lasts a fishing season of daily use in sun and water | Medium | Fisher association |
| UR-010 | Deployment and assembly | Follows the shore | We can move it as the water rises and falls | Medium | Fisher association |
| UR-011 | Cost | Affordable | A household or association can pay for a station | High | Fisher association; NGOs |
| UR-012 | Maintenance | Made and mended locally | A local welder, carpenter and rope worker can make and mend it from parts they can buy | Medium | Station builder |
| UR-013 | Deployment and assembly | Installed without wading | The far end can be placed from a boat or at low water by two people | High | Boat crew; wildlife authority |
| UR-014 | Regulatory | Allowed where we fish | The station and the way we fish with it are allowed by the wildlife and fisheries authorities | Medium | Wildlife and fisheries authorities |

Categories: deployment and assembly, performance, usability, safety, environment and durability, logistics, maintenance, cost, regulatory. Priorities: High, Medium, Low.
